# Medina College Ipil, Incorporated — Campus Information & Alert Center

**Live Demo:** https://lealipayo.github.io/-Event-sign-Up-Sheet/  
**Repository:** https://github.com/lealipayo/-Event-sign-Up-Sheet

## Community Problem
Students, faculty, and administrative staff at **Medina College Ipil, Incorporated** (Ipil, Zamboanga Sibugay) often rely on scattered group chats, word-of-mouth, or printed bulletin boards for urgent campus announcements, class suspensions, weather advisories, and organizational notices. Important updates easily get buried in long chat threads, causing missed deadlines, confusion during weather emergencies, and uncoordinated campus communication.

## Functional Requirement 01 (FR-01)
**FR-01 — Post, Categorize, and Track Campus Advisories:**  
Authorized campus staff and student leaders can compose and publish official campus notices with a **Category** (*Emergency / Weather Alert*, *Class / Office Advisory*, *Event / Org Notice*, *Health & Safety*), **Priority Level** (*Urgent*, *Important*, *General Info*), **Title**, **Details**, **Target Department / Course**, **Expiry / Effective Date**, and a **Pin to Top** option. Users can filter notices by category or active status, search keywords in real time, resolve active alerts, and view live summary counters—all persisted locally in the browser via `localStorage`.

## How to Run
1. Open `https://lealipayo.github.io/-Event-sign-Up-Sheet/` in any desktop or mobile browser, **or**
2. Clone the repository and open `index.html` directly in your browser (no backend server required).
